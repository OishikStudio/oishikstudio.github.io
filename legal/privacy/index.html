Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 5 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Oishik Studio ("we", "our", "the Site") handles information when you visit our website. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process any personal information directly through this website, such as names, email addresses, or account details. This Site does not use forms, logins, or e-commerce features that would require you to submit personal data.

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics, a web analytics service provided by Google LLC ("Google"), to help us understand how visitors use our Site. Google Analytics may collect information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the Site, typically through the use of cookies.

This data is collected and processed by Google on our behalf. We do not have access to any personally identifiable information beyond the aggregated, anonymized statistics that Google Analytics provides to us.

For more information on how Google collects and processes data, please review Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

3. Cookies

Google Analytics may place cookies on your device to collect the usage data described above. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Please note that disabling cookies may limit certain analytics functionality but will not affect your ability to use this Site.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties, other than the data automatically processed by Google as described above under the terms of the Google Analytics service.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR or CCPA) regarding data collected via Google Analytics, including the right to opt out. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on .

6. Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
